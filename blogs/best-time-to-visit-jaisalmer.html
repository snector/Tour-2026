Jaisalmer kab jaayein? Best months, weather aur crowds

Thar desert mein timing hi experience banati hai — cool nights, soft dunes aur open camps ke liye sahi window choose karo.

Jaisalmer saal bhar photogenic hai, lekin guest experience October se March ke beech sabse smooth rehta hai. Is window mein din warm-pleasant hote hain, raatein campfire ke liye perfect, aur desert safari uncomfortable nahi lagti.

Best window: October–March

Is season mein temperature usually 15–28°C ke around rehta hai. Hotels aur desert camps pehle se fill ho jaate hain — especially December–January aur Desert Festival weeks. Agar aap commission booking le rahe ho, to guests ko 3–4 hafte pehle package lock karwane ko kaho.

  • Oct–Nov: Soft crowds, clear skies, great for first-timers.
  • Dec–Jan: Peak romance + peak prices. Book early.
  • Feb–Mar: Warming up, still excellent for dunes aur fort walks.

Summer (Apr–Jun) — sirf experienced travelers ke liye

April ke baad afternoon heat intense ho jaati hai. Early morning fort walks aur late evening camel rides possible hain, lekin mid-day outdoor plans avoid karo. Budget travelers ke liye deals milti hain — bas heat expectations clear rakho.

Monsoon (Jul–Sep)

Light rains se city soft green dikhti hai, lekin dune camps kabhi-kabhi close/limited rehte hain. Road trips possible hain, desert overnight packages confirm karke hi sell karo.

Booking tip for commission partners

Peak dates pe 3-Day Heritage package sabse zyada close hota hai — higher ticket, higher cut (~18%). Shoulder months mein Desert Overnight upsell easy rehta hai.

Sahi month decide ho gaya? Ab package lock karo.

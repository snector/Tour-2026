Perfect 3-day Jaisalmer itinerary

Ek balanced plan jo first-timers ko overwhelm nahi karta — aur seedha hamare best-seller package se match karta hai.

Teen din Jaisalmer ke liye sweet spot hain: ek din city soul, ek din surroundings, ek din desert immersion. Yeh itinerary Sonar Trails 3-Day Heritage package (~18% commission) ka blueprint hai.

Day 1 — Golden city immersion

  • Morning: Hotel check-in, light breakfast
  • Late morning–afternoon: Sonar Qila guided walk + Jain temples
  • Evening: Patwon ki Haveli, Gadisar Lake boating / sunset
  • Night: Market stroll + Rajasthani thali

Day 2 — Stories beyond the fort

  • Morning: Kuldhara abandoned village
  • Afternoon: Bada Bagh / local craft stop (optional)
  • Transfer to Sam dunes
  • Sunset camel safari → cultural program → camp dinner → overnight

Day 3 — Soft landing

  • Sunrise over dunes (optional early wake-up)
  • Breakfast at camp, return to city
  • Souvenir time, drop to hotel/station/airport

Who is this for?

Couples, small families, aur friends groups. Agar guest ke paas sirf 1–2 din hain, to Golden Fort Day ya Desert Overnight pe down-sell karo — better conversion, zyada honest experience.

Packing micro-list

Sunglasses, scarf (dust), warm layer for winter nights, comfortable walking shoes, power bank. Desert camps mein card machines kabhi fail hoti hain — thoda cash rakho.

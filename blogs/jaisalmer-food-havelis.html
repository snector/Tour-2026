Haveli stays aur street food jo miss nahi karna

Jaisalmer sirf dunes nahi — carved balconies, thalis aur chai stalls bhi memory banate hain.

Guests aksar fort aur safari pe focus karte hain, lekin stay aur food hi trip ko premium feel dete hain. Commission partners ke liye yeh upsell goldmine hai: heritage room + food trail add-on.

Haveli / heritage stay vibes

City ke andar sandstone havelis carved jharokhas aur courtyards ke saath aati hain. Fort-view rooms thode mehenghe hote hain — honeymoon bookings mein easy close. Quiet lanes choose karo agar guest light sleeper hai; main market ke bilkul upar kabhi noisy ho sakta hai.

Must-eat list

  • Dal baati churma — classic Rajasthani comfort
  • Ker sangri — desert berries & beans, local signature
  • Mirchi bada — evening snack with chai
  • Gatte ki sabzi — gram-flour dumplings in gravy
  • Laal maas (non-veg lovers) — spicy mutton, portion size confirm karo

Where to eat smart

Fort ke andar rooftop cafés view ke liye perfect hain, lekin prices tourist-side hote hain. Local thalis ke liye market area try karo. Hygiene ke liye busy stalls prefer karo — turnover high = fresher food.

Partner tip

3-Day Heritage package mein stay tier upgrade offer karo (standard → haveli suite). Chhoti si difference pe guest khush, aapki effective commission better.

Food + fort + dunes — poora circuit ek booking mein.

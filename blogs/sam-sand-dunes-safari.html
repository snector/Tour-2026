Sam Sand Dunes camel safari: honest guide

Thar ki classic night — bina overpriced camp aur fake “VIP” packages ke.

Sam dunes Jaisalmer se roughly 40–45 km hain. Sunset camel ride, Rajasthani dinner, folk music aur starry sky — yeh combo hi desert overnight ka core hai. Lekin market crowded hai, isliye quality filter zaroori hai.

Camel vs jeep

  • Camel: Slow, iconic, photos ke liye best. 30–60 min ride enough hai.
  • Jeep: Thrill seekers ke liye. Dusty, loud — couple romantic vibe ke liye optional rakho.

Camp types — seedha bolna

Swiss tents / deluxe camps attached washroom aur better bedding dete hain. Basic tents budget-friendly hain lekin shared facilities. Honeymoon ya first-time guests ko deluxe recommend karo — cancellation kam, reviews better, commission bhi healthy.

Tourist traps se bacho

City gate pe “cheapest desert” bechne wale agents se seedha deal mat lo. Package mein clearly likho: pickup, camel duration, dinner inclusions, cultural show, aur next-day drop. Hidden add-ons (bonfire charge, cultural show tip) pehle clear karo.

Best timing

Camp check-in usually afternoon. Sunset se pehle dunes pe pahunchna ideal hai. Winter raaton mein warm layer le jaana — desert raat mein thanda ho jaata hai.
